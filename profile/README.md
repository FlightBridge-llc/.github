# FlightBridge

FlightBridge is the trip logistics platform for private aviation. Flight departments, charter operators and FBOs use it to book crew hotels at negotiated rates, rental cars with FBO delivery, airline tickets, FBO and handler services, and catering. More than 50 integrations with scheduling and FBO systems keep every booking in step with the flight schedule. Over 40,000 private aviation professionals use FlightBridge.

## FlightBridge for AI assistants

The FlightBridge connector lets an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT search and book through your own FlightBridge account. You sign in with your FlightBridge credentials, and the assistant works within your existing permissions. There are two plugins here. Each brings the connector together with the skill for one kind of business:

- FlightBridge for FBOs, for customer service reps booking for visiting aircraft.
- FlightBridge for Aircraft Operators, for flight departments, charter schedulers and Trip Managers.

Setup for each assistant: https://poc-developer.flightbridge.com/connect
